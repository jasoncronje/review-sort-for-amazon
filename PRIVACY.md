# Privacy policy: Review Sort for Amazon

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Review Sort for Amazon collects no personal data.

- It has no servers and sends nothing to the developer or to anyone else.
- It runs only on Amazon sites. It reads the search results page you are on and, when you choose more than one page, requests the next result pages from that same Amazon site, inside your browser.
- Your settings and saved defaults are kept in your browser's local storage for that Amazon site. Removing the extension or clearing site data deletes them.
- It uses no analytics, no cookies of its own and no remote code.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/jasoncronje/review-sort-for-amazon/issues
